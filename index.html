How to use it

1. Play until it is your turn.
2. Screenshot the entire phone screen.
3. Upload the screenshot here.
4. Tap the highlighted tile in your game.

This is an assistive analyzer, not an automatic clicker. It cannot see hidden tile artwork until that tile is exposed.
